contact sheet (`<id>.png`, made with `node scripts/contact-sheet.mjs <id> acceptance/<id>.png`) with the time printed under
every tile. The five searches per video and the second ranges where the answer is visible are in `queries.json`, written
from the sheets before any search was run. Tolerance is 2 s on each side of a range.

## Gate

After the full-detail read, the right moment in the top 5 for at least 14 of 15 searches and first for at least 11 of 15
(the plan's 90 and 70 percent, on 15 searches).

## Known weak spots, written down before any search

- Launch video: several crowd and control-room shots look alike, and many frames are almost black; the searches avoid
  those scenes. The landing is shown twice (about 2:10 far away, about 2:44 close); the landing-legs search only lists
  the close one.
- Game video: the blue-sky level appears three times (0:10, 2:04, 2:24); no search targets it.
- Cooking video: the presenter's face fills many tiles, and fennel, bread and meat in the pan look similar from a distance.
