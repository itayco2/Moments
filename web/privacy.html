Moments privacy

Moments (the website and the Chrome extension "Moments: search YouTube by what you see") runs its AI model on your own computer. It has no servers that receive your data, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension reads

On YouTube video pages it reads the video's id, length and YouTube's own preview images, and, when you search a video for the first time, the frames of the video in your player. It turns them into numbers on your computer.

That first search pauses your player and steps it through the video to read its frames in full detail, then puts it back at the same second. Skip or Esc stops it at any time and keeps what was read.

What it stores

Those numbers, per video, in your browser (at most 200 MB, oldest dropped first), your background-reading setting, and the AI model files. "Forget saved videos" in the extension's menu deletes the numbers.

What leaves your computer

Nothing you type into the search bar, and nothing about you. The extension makes two kinds of downloads:

  • The AI model, about 400 MB, once, from moments-video.vercel.app (or Hugging Face as a backup). The download starts at your first search (or when you open a video, if you turn background reading on in the extension's menu).
  • YouTube's preview images of the video you are watching, from YouTube's image server (i.ytimg.com). Each request names that video, the same images YouTube's own player loads, and is sent without your cookies. With background reading off (the default), they are downloaded only for a video you search.

The website

moments-video.vercel.app is hosted by Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel's servers see your IP address and the pages you request (server logs, under Vercel's privacy policy). The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or ads, and its fonts are served from the site itself.

The demo videos stream from NASA's servers (images-assets.nasa.gov). Videos you add from your own computer never leave it: they, the numbers made from them and the AI model are kept in your browser's storage for this site, and clearing the site's data removes them.
