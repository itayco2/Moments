Moments privacy

Moments (the website and the Chrome extension "Moments: search YouTube by what you see") runs its AI model on your own computer. It has no servers that receive your data, no accounts, no analytics and no ads.

What the extension reads

On YouTube video pages it reads the video's id, length and YouTube's own preview images, and, when you search a video for the first time, the frames of the video in your player. It turns them into numbers on your computer.

What it stores

Those numbers, per video, in your browser (at most 200 MB, oldest dropped first), your background-reading setting, and the AI model files. "Forget saved videos" in the extension's menu deletes the numbers.

What leaves your computer

Nothing about you or your videos. The extension downloads the AI model once (from moments-video.vercel.app, or Hugging Face as a backup) and YouTube's preview images from YouTube. These are ordinary downloads that do not include anything you searched or watched.
