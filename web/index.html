How it works

Sorted by meaning

Moments reads a frame from every few seconds of the video. Frames that look alike land close together, so a two-hour broadcast becomes a map you can search with a sentence.

  1. 01Every frame becomes 768 numbersThe model looks at the picture itself. No transcript, no tags.
  2. 02Your words become numbers tooSame model, same 768 numbers, so a sentence and a frame can be compared.
  3. 03The closest frame winsThe player jumps there, and every other match is marked on the progress bar.

After the first search

Ask again. It is instant.

The first search reads the video in full detail once, then saves what it learned on your computer. Every search after that answers before you lift your finger from Enter.

Free, open source

Install in one minute

Moments is not in the Chrome Web Store, so you load it yourself. Four clicks, then open any YouTube video and the bar sits under the player.

Download for Chrome

moments-youtube.zip, 7.2 MB. Details

  1. 01Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. 02Open chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. 03Turn on Developer mode, top right.
  4. 04Click Load unpacked and pick the unzipped folder.
