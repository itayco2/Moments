Get the extension

Moments for YouTube adds a search bar under the player. Type what you want to see and the video jumps to that second.

Download for Chrome

Free. Chrome 124 or newer, Edge or Brave. The first use downloads the model once (about 400 MB). Details

  1. 01Download the zip and unzip it.
  2. 02Open chrome://extensions in the address bar.
  3. 03Turn on Developer mode (top right).
  4. 04Click Load unpacked and choose the unzipped folder.
How it works

Three steps, all inside your browser tab.

01

It reads the video

One frame every 4 seconds, taken by your browser. The file never leaves your computer.

02

Each frame becomes 768 numbers

Google's open EmbeddingGemma 2 describes what is in the frame, running on your own GPU.

03

Your words find the closest frame

Your sentence becomes numbers the same way. The nearest moment plays from that second.
